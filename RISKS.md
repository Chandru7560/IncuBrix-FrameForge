# ScenePilot AI Risks and Mitigations

Risk register for the current implementation. The initial workspace contained only the supplied assessment materials; API, planning, retrieval, and rendering slices have since been added. Severity reflects potential impact; status is open unless explicitly stated.

| Risk | Severity | Status / evidence | Mitigation and verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local Compose and external object-store behavior are not acceptance-tested. | High | Docker is unavailable; Compose definitions have not been started. Output files currently use a shared local volume; MinIO is provisioned but not used for artifacts. | Verify clean Compose startup, persistent volumes, readiness, and MinIO/S3 uploads in a Docker-capable environment before deployment. |
| No configured footage source currently supplies sufficient commercial-rights and watermark evidence. | Critical | Pexels/Pixabay adapters are implemented but have no credentials in this workspace; metadata lacks asset-level watermark verification, and the selector rejects unknown status. Local fixtures are synthetic and non-publishable. | Keep publishable rendering blocked unless an adapter/verifier supplies applicable asset evidence and explicit no-watermark status; recheck current terms and record evidence. Never treat search success or operator attestation as per-asset proof. |
| A test fixture or fallback could be mistaken for genuine B-roll. | High | The local adapter provides synthetic metadata only, labels fixtures, and rights/render policy rejects unverified/test material. | Preserve the explicit non-publishable marker and fail if there is no eligible asset; never substitute a simulated output for real footage. |
| Uploaded speech cannot be transcribed, and scripts do not yet receive actual spoken narration. | High | Adapter protocols, WAV probing, deterministic contextual planning, and injected-adapter tests exist; default mock STT refuses to fabricate transcripts, and mock TTS generates only a silent timing fixture. No live provider is wired into worker startup. | Add an approved real adapter when credentials and terms are available. Until then, clearly disclose this limitation and reject rendering without genuine narration. |
| Long-running jobs can be lost, duplicated, or report success before durable outputs exist. | High | Persistent jobs/idempotency, worker lifecycle, artifact allowlisting, file validation, and concurrent-retry tests are implemented. | Keep terminal success gated on renderer output validation; verify worker recovery and output-volume persistence across process/container restarts. |
| URL ingestion can expose SSRF, internal services, DNS rebinding, unbounded downloads, redirects, or malicious media. | High | Audio URL worker ingestion validates public HTTPS targets, pins the TLS connection to a validated public IP, revalidates each redirect, bounds time/bytes/duration, validates format, and cleans partial files. Tests cover mixed public/private DNS, redirect checks, and byte-limit cleanup. Production egress isolation and resource sandboxing remain unverified. | Keep the worker behind restricted egress and resource limits; preserve pinned-IP/redirect/byte/duration checks, isolate probing of untrusted media, and re-run the SSRF regression tests before deployment. |
| Malformed or adversarial media can exhaust CPU, memory, disk, or worker slots. | High | Upload and processing limits, subprocess timeouts, bounded downloads, FFprobe validation, and cleanup paths are implemented. Resource isolation and production concurrency limits remain deployment work. | Run media workers in resource-limited containers; cap concurrency/disk, isolate probing, retain timeouts, and test corrupt/oversized inputs. |
| Content may depict real people, brands, vulnerable communities, or sensitive events in unsafe or misleading ways. | High | Narrative intent and retrieval carry avoid/safety metadata, but there is no independent comprehensive content-safety classifier or human review workflow. | Use neutral/verified assets, apply explicit avoid constraints, preserve warnings, and require human review for sensitive content before publication. |
| Scene matching can overfit keywords, fail on ambiguity/abstraction, or obscure subject framing in 9:16. | Medium–High | Deterministic scene interpretation and weighted retrieval are tested; an optional vision-language evaluator and rating collection exist, but no real evaluator or human-rating evidence exists. Subject detection is optional; default framing is deterministic center-crop. | Preserve context and selection rationales; use an injected detector when available; measure human relevance and framing rather than treating keyword score as quality. |
| Rendering may produce gaps, audio drift, black/frozen frames, inconsistent colour, or incorrect aspect crops. | High | FFmpeg renderer performs stream/duration/decode/coverage checks and measurable black/freeze checks; perceived brightness, subject-safe composition without an injected detector, and reliable audio intelligibility remain unmeasured. | Run the real-binary integration test for each supported ratio and inspect outputs; report unavailable checks and do not overstate subjective or unmeasurable quality. |
| Licence metadata can be incomplete, change, or fail to prove commercial usage, attribution, or no-watermark status. | Critical | Rights passports and fail-closed selection/render guards are implemented; current external adapters cannot provide sufficient watermark evidence. | Treat missing/ambiguous rights as ineligible; retain provider response, source terms, licence/evidence URLs, attribution, retrieval/verification time, and watermark assessment; revalidate before real deployment. |
| Credentials, user audio, or sensitive inputs may leak in logs or artifacts. | High | Credentials are environment-driven and structured log fields are restricted; no external provider has been configured. | Continue using secret management and allowlisted logging; test redaction with real integrations and restrict artifact-volume access/retention. |
| External relevance evaluation can disclose selected media frames and narration, and unauthenticated ratings can be manipulated. | High | Evaluator is disabled by default, sends selected-video frames plus narration/intent only when configured, and reports null/unavailable on failure. Rating endpoints currently have no authentication. | Review endpoint retention/terms and user consent before enabling; restrict evaluator network egress, add authentication/authorization and audit controls before public deployment, and treat submitted ratings as untrusted until reviewed. |
| Callback delivery may fail or signatures may be computed over the wrong payload. | Medium | Optional callback delivery is not implemented. | Canonicalize signed bytes, use HMAC-SHA256 and constant-time validation, bound retries, and test exact signatures before exposing callbacks. |
| Cost, throughput, relevance, and reviewer-quality figures may be guessed or overclaimed. | High | No production benchmark, cost data, or reviewer results exist; the manifest records costs as unavailable unless supplied. | Benchmark with stated hardware, workloads, repetitions, and provider pricing; preserve raw results; report `not measured` when evidence is absent. |
| The supplied workbook may encourage premature completion claims. | Medium | A technical draft records current evidence, partial/not-achieved items, and measured test results. The original workbook is preserved; personal details and declaration/signature fields remain blank. | Keep the draft unsigned; fill private/personal fields and declaration answers only after the owner reviews every claim and confirms them. |
| The live demo URL, availability period, repository, user contact details, and deployment credentials are not present. | Medium | A GitHub target/profile and author email were supplied, but Git/GitHub CLI are not installed, there is no local `.git` repository, and no create/push integration is available. Search returned no matching public repository; a private repository could not be verified. No Google Drive desktop sync/upload integration was detected. | Do not claim a repository or Drive upload exists. Install Git/GitHub CLI, authenticate with the configured account, verify the target repository's visibility/contents, then publish the reviewed tree. Ask before changing visibility or copying the archive to a synced Drive folder. |

## Risk review trigger

Revisit this register after a provider/verifier is configured, an end-to-end publishable render is validated, and before live deployment. Escalate any unverified commercial-rights or safety issue to a blocking condition for publishable outputs.
